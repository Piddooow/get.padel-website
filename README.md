# get.padel-website

Get Padel Jakarta

The official website for Get Padel Jakarta, an indoor padel venue in Pondok Kelapa, East Jakarta.

Get Padel helps players find court information, plan a session, review pricing and programs, and book through the official AYO channel.

About

Get Padel Jakarta offers two indoor padel courts for casual games, training sessions, and community play. The venue also includes Race & Rally Coffee, giving players a place to prepare before a match or recharge afterward.
